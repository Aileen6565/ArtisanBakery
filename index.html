Our Story

Flour, Water, Salt, Time. Nothing Else.

At Levain & Hearth, bread is not an industrial commodity—it is living biology, respectful agriculture, and an unbroken communion between the soil and your table.

Our bakery started eight years ago with an antique glass jar of wild sourdough culture affectionately christened "Clara", captured from the wild yeasts drifting through local orchard blossoms. Today, Clara remains the living mother starter behind every single loaf we bake.

We collaborate solely with regional Pacific Northwest organic grain farms practicing regenerative soil stewardship. Their heritage grain varietals are slowly stone-ground at cool temperatures so the nutritious germ, aromatic bran oils, and natural enzymes remain fully alive.

By giving our dough 36 to 40 hours of cold retard in willow proofing baskets, the wild microbes gently break down complex starches and gluten into easily digestible nourishment and nuanced, unforgettable flavor.

Stone-Milled Heritage Wheat

Ancient grain varietals rich in minerals, micronutrients, and authentic wheaten perfumes.

The Art of Slow Ferment

Extended cold fermentation unlocks digestible nutrition and deep caramelized crust blister.

— Matthieu Vance, Founder & Head Baker
